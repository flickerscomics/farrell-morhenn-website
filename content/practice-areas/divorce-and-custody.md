Divorce involves legal, financial and emotional decisions at once. We guide clients through the dissolution process, the division of marital property and the arrangements for children's care and contact.

Where parents can agree, we help them reach and formalise a parenting plan that will hold. Where they cannot, we prepare a clear, evidence-based case for the court.
