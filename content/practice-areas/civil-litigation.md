Civil disputes in Los Santos range from unpaid invoices to complex commercial disagreements. We represent claimants and defendants in negotiations, demand correspondence and proceedings before the San Andreas civil courts.

Litigation is rarely the first or best option. We assess the strength of a claim, the realistic prospects of recovery and the cost of getting there before recommending a course of action. Many matters resolve through a well-prepared letter or a structured settlement discussion.

When a dispute does go to court, we handle pleadings, discovery, motions and hearings, and we keep you informed at each stage.

## If you are being sued

If you have received a demand letter or court papers, the deadlines to respond are short, and missing them can mean a judgment is entered against you. We review the claim, advise on your defences and any counterclaims, and respond on your behalf, whether that means negotiating an early resolution or defending the case through trial.
