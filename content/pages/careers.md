We are a small firm, which means everyone's work matters and everyone is known by name. New attorneys handle real matters early, with a partner always available for guidance. Paralegals and administrative staff are part of the team, not an afterthought.

We look for people who write clearly, prepare carefully and treat clients and colleagues with respect. Experience is welcome but character counts for more.
