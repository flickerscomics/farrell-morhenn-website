## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no forms or accounts. We do not collect personal information through it. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security purposes.

## When you contact us

If you call, email or visit the office, we use the information you give us to respond to you, check for conflicts of interest and, if we act for you, to provide our services. We treat everything you tell us as confidential.

## How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and client records only for as long as we need them to provide our services and meet our professional obligations.

## Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting the office.
