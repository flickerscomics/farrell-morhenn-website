  - heading: Our history
    body: |-
      Farrell-Morhenn began in 2016 as a two-attorney criminal defence practice above a pharmacy in Little Seoul, built on a simple idea: clients should always know where they stand.

      The firm added civil litigation and business law in 2018 and has grown steadily since, adding family, property and personal injury practices. In 2021 it moved to the Arcadius Business Center in Pillbox Hill.
  - heading: Mission
    body: To give the people and businesses of Los Santos honest legal advice and determined representation, whatever the size of their matter.
  - heading: Our values
    body: |-
      - **Candour.** We tell clients what we think, including when it is not what they hoped to hear.
      - **Preparation.** Good outcomes come from knowing the file better than anyone else in the room.
      - **Discretion.** What clients tell us stays with us.
      - **Accessibility.** Plain language, clear fees agreed in advance and a lawyer who returns calls.
  - heading: How we work with clients
    body: Every matter has a named attorney who is responsible for it. At the start of an engagement we agree the scope of the work, how we will communicate and what it will cost. We keep clients informed at each significant step and explain the options before decisions are made.
