Residents of Blaine County often travel to Los Santos for even the simplest legal question. Starting next month, the firm will hold a free walk-in clinic in Paleto Bay on the first Saturday of every month.

## Details

- **Where:** Paleto Bay community centre
- **When:** First Saturday of the month, 11:00 to 14:00 Pacific Time
- **Who:** Any resident with a general legal question

Clinic sessions offer general information only. If a matter needs further work, we will explain how to make a formal enquiry.
