Farrell-Morhenn today announced that [url=/people/grace-ashworth][b]Associate Attorney Grace Ashworth[/b][/url], who already advises on wills, estates and property, will now also take family matters, joining the firm's established family practice.

Demand for family law advice has grown steadily over the past year. The additional capacity means clients can expect:

[list]
[*][b]Faster first meetings[/b]: most initial consultations offered within five working days
[*]A named attorney responsible for each matter from start to finish
[*]Consultations available in [i]English and Spanish[/i]
[/list]

[h2]Matters the team handles[/h2]
The family team advises on divorce and separation, custody and visitation arrangements, child support, and residency questions. It also works closely with the firm's [url=/practice-areas/estate-planning-and-probate]estate planning[/url] attorneys where a family matter affects a will or an estate.

[quote=Grace Ashworth, Associate Attorney]Family matters and estate planning often go hand in hand. I'm glad to be working with a team that takes the time to explain where clients stand before anything else happens.[/quote]
