Residents of Blaine County often travel to Los Santos for even the simplest legal question. From November, Farrell-Morhenn will hold a [b]free walk-in legal clinic[/b] in Paleto Bay on the first Saturday of every month.

[h2]Upcoming sessions[/h2]

[tr][th]Date[/th][th]Time[/th][th]Location[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Saturday 7 November[/td][td]11:00 – 14:00[/td][td]Paleto Bay community centre[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Saturday 5 December[/td][td]11:00 – 14:00[/td][td]Paleto Bay community centre[/td][/tr]
[/table]

No appointment is needed. Clinic sessions offer general information only; where a matter needs further work, attorneys will explain how to make a formal enquiry with the firm.
