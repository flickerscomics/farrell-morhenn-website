Farrell-Morhenn & Associates has officially initiated a litigation against the owner of the "Dolls of Los Santos" account, spearheaded by attorney Salvador "Savior" Moreno. The account in question was dedicated to snapping pictures of unknowing women in public, describing them as "dolls", and the act as capturing and immortalizing their beauty; its goal was to establish a "personal collection"[cite: 1]. The creepy nature of the posts brought a lot of attention online, which served to invoke severe emotional distress in one of the girls pictured, who has since come forward[cite: 1].

Needless to say, the woman is now suing for negligent infliction of emotional distress, and infliction of emotional distress, as a result of the outrageous, creepy, and borderline fetishistic conduct of the defendant that has since left her with incredible anxiety[cite: 1]. Salvadore Moreno, Associate at Farrell-Morhenn & Associates, said[cite: 1]:

Statement from [b]ATTORNEY SALVADOR MORENO[/b]:

[quote]"This case is about more than photographs[cite: 1]. It's not about whether you can take a picture of someone in a public space[cite: 1]. It's about the deliberate target of women to be added to someone's so-called collection—my client deserves to go about her life without fear of being watched, photographed, and publicly displayed for the entertainment of some fetishistic stranger[cite: 1]. This has only caused her intense, severe, emotional distress[cite: 1]. We intend to identify and hold the individual behind the account accountable through the proper legal process."[/quote]

Farrell-Morhenn remains steadfast in its commitment to promote the public interest[cite: 1].[/font]
