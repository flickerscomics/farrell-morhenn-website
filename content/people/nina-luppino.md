Nina Luppino practices criminal law, traffic law, business and civil litigation, representing clients in legal disputes and advising businesses on their legal obligations and interests. Her work is guided by a commitment to resolving outstanding matters, protecting her clients' rights and pursuing justice where an injustice has occurred.

Raised in Downtown, Los Santos, Nina brings a firm understanding of the city's legal landscape to her practice. Fluent in both Italian and English, she works with clients from diverse backgrounds, providing legal counsel and representation with professionalism, discretion and determination.
