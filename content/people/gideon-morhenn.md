Gideon Morhenn is a Partner at Farrell-Morhenn, practicing criminal, civil and corporate law.

Gideon earned a Bachelor of Arts in Government from Harvard College and a Juris Doctor from Harvard Law School. Before practicing law he served as a Senior Cabinet Advisor in the Los Santos City Government, an experience that gives him a close understanding of how public bodies make decisions. He later worked as a paralegal at Farrell-Marino & Associates and was admitted to the State Bar of San Andreas in 2025.

He works with clients in English, Kurdish and Turkish.
