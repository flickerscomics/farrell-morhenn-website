Marty Farrell is the Managing Partner of Farrell-Morhenn. He practices criminal law, civil law, and business and corporate law, and oversees the firm's work for clients across Los Santos.

Marty earned a Bachelor of Arts from Chicago State University and a Juris Doctor from Pacific Coast University School of Law. During law school he clerked at the Cook County State's Attorney's Office, gaining early experience of how criminal cases are prepared and brought to court. He works with clients in English and Spanish.
